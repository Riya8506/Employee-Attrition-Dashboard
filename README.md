# Employee Attrition Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Excel project analyzes employee attrition data to identify patterns
in employee turnover across departments, job roles, age groups, salary
levels, business travel, overtime, job satisfaction, and employee
tenure.

The dashboard converts employee-level HR data into an interactive view
of attrition patterns and supporting workforce insights.

## Dataset

-   1,480 employee records
-   27 fields in the main data table
-   Department, job role, age group, salary information, business
    travel, overtime, job satisfaction, marital status, and
    tenure-related fields
-   Attrition status and an attrition flag used for analysis

## Business Questions

-   How many employees are in the dataset and how much attrition is
    occurring?
-   How does attrition vary by department?
-   How does attrition vary by job role?
-   Which age groups show different attrition rates?
-   How does attrition vary across salary slabs?
-   How does business travel relate to attrition rates?
-   How does overtime relate to attrition?
-   How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
-   How does years at the company relate to attrition?

## Dashboard Analysis

The workbook includes analysis of:

-   Overall employee and attrition KPIs
-   Attrition by department
-   Attrition by job role
-   Attrition by age group
-   Attrition by salary slab
-   Attrition by business travel frequency
-   Attrition by overtime status
-   Attrition by job satisfaction
-   Attrition by years at company / tenure slab

The workbook also contains interactive slicers for selected employee
attributes and a separate **Business Insights Page**.

## Business Insights

A separate Business Insights page is included in the workbook to present
the deeper observations from the dashboard analysis.

The analysis describes relationships and patterns in the dataset; it
should not be interpreted as proving that an individual factor directly
causes employee attrition.
